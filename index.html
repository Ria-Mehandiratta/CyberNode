🛡️ AI Cybersecurity Guidelines & Threat Defense

Autonomous recommendations generated by the CyberNode Threat Engine

Evil Twin Attack Prevention

Attackers spin up a clone AP with the identical SSID of your college or coffee shop. CyberNode compares BSSID MAC addresses against known baseline profiles to detect impersonation instantly.

Open Public Wi-Fi Risks

Unencrypted networks expose plaintext wireless frames to nearby radio listeners. Never perform bank transactions or enter credentials while connected to an Open AP without CyberShield enabled.

Always Prefer WPA3 / Modern Ciphers

WPA3 uses Simultaneous Authentication of Equals (SAE), rendering offline dictionary and handshake cracking ineffective. Avoid legacy WEP or outdated TKIP ciphers.

Signal Strength RSSI Heuristics

An unknown access point boasting an abnormally high signal (> -45 dBm) located indoors could indicate a portable rogue Wi-Fi pineapple or attack box placed near you.
